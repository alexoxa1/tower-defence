# Citadel Watch

A Watch is a defense of the citadel along a single road. Enemies walk from In to Out. Towers occupy open ground beside that road.

## Board

**Board**:
The playable ground around the road. Towers sit at free points on it.
_Avoid_: Map, battlefield, hex grid, tile, cell

**Road**:
The fixed lane enemies walk from In to Out.
_Avoid_: Path, track, lane

**In**:
The portal at the start of the road.
_Avoid_: Spawn, entrance, gate

**Out**:
The portal at the end of the road.
_Avoid_: Exit, leak point, base, core

**Citadel**:
What the Watch defends. It has no body on the board.
_Avoid_: Core, nexus, HQ, keep

## Towers

**Armory**:
The catalog of tower types the player can place.
_Avoid_: Shop, store, loadout

**Tower**:
A placed defense on the board. It has a type, a level, and a point.
_Avoid_: Battery, unit, turret, building

**Scout**:
The stance with no tower type selected.
_Avoid_: Pan mode, inspect, view mode

**Hex Gun**:
The fast, short-range tower.
_Avoid_: Basic, hex tile

**Mortar Post**:
The splash tower.
_Avoid_: Mortar, Cannon

**Rail Sniper**:
The long-range tower.
_Avoid_: Rail, Sniper

**Level**:
The rank of a placed tower, from 1 to 10.
_Avoid_: Tier, rank, star

## Enemies

**Enemy**:
A hostile walking the road.
_Avoid_: Mob, unit, foe, generic creep

**Creep**:
The baseline enemy kind.

**Runner**:
A fast, fragile enemy kind.

**Brute**:
A slow, heavy enemy kind.

**Swarm**:
A small, cheap enemy kind.

**Warden**:
A heavy mid-campaign enemy kind.

**Shade**:
A fast elite enemy kind.

**Colossus**:
A boss. An escape costs two lives.

**Overlord**:
The heaviest boss. An escape costs three lives.

**Boss**:
A Colossus or Overlord attached to a wave as its last arrival.
_Avoid_: Mini-boss, elite

**Escape**:
An enemy reaching Out still alive.
_Avoid_: Leak, breach, finish

## Combat

**Projectile**:
A shot in the air, aimed at one enemy.
_Avoid_: Bullet, bolt, missile

**Splash**:
The blast radius of a Mortar Post projectile.
_Avoid_: AoE, area damage

**Slow**:
A timed speed penalty on an enemy. Rail Sniper applies it.
_Avoid_: Freeze, stun, snare, root

**Range**:
A tower's targeting distance.
_Avoid_: Radius, aura

**Combo**:
A streak of kills close together in time.
_Avoid_: Multiplier, chain

## Economy

**Gold**:
The currency of a Watch.
_Avoid_: Money, cash, coin

**Lives**:
Remaining endurance of the citadel. An escape spends one or more.
_Avoid_: HP, health, core HP

**Reward**:
Gold paid for killing an enemy.
_Avoid_: Bounty, drop, loot

**Clear bonus**:
Gold paid when a wave has no enemies left and none left to arrive.
_Avoid_: Wave reward, completion gold

**Spent**:
Gold already put into one tower, place plus upgrades.

**Refund**:
Gold returned on sell, a portion of Spent.
_Avoid_: Salvage

**Score**:
Points from kills, combo, and clear bonuses. Not a currency.
_Avoid_: XP

## Match

**Watch**:
One play from a fresh board until Rift Broken or a reset.
_Avoid_: Game, run, session, match

**Wave**:
A named assault with a roster and sometimes a boss.
_Avoid_: Round, level, stage

**Campaign**:
The first fifteen named waves. Later waves reuse those plans with more enemies.
_Avoid_: Mission, chapter, act

**Hold**:
The named idle before wave 1. Not a pause.
_Avoid_: Ready

**Rift Broken**:
The lost Watch. Lives have reached zero.
_Avoid_: Game over, Fallen

**Speed**:
How fast the Watch runs, 1x through 5x.
_Avoid_: Timescale, playback
